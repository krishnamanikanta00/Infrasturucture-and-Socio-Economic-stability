Problem Statement

The Housing Gap

Students and early-career professionals often choose housing far from work or college because nearby accommodation is expensive. Long commutes increase stress, reduce productivity, create financial pressure, and affect quality of life.

🏠
Housing Affordability
Rent near urban colleges and offices consumes 30–50% of a student's monthly budget.
🚌
Transportation Burden
Daily commutes of 1–3 hours drain time and energy critical for learning and work.
💸
Financial Stress
Balancing rent, food, tuition and transport leaves minimal savings or breathing room.
😔
Reduced Quality of Life
Fatigue, poor nutrition, and lack of social life follow from housing-commute tradeoffs.

Solutions & Opportunities

How We Can Help

Human-centered interventions addressing the root causes of housing stress for students and young professionals.

01
Smart Housing Recommendation System
AI-powered platform matching budget, commute distance, and lifestyle preferences for personalized suggestions.
02
Commute-Time Calculator
Integrated tool showing real-time travel times to college or office from any listed property.
03
Verified Housing Listings
Curated, verified PG and flat listings with transparent pricing, photos, and owner verification.
04
Roommate Matching
Profile-based compatibility matching to help students find trustworthy, compatible roommates nearby.
05
Affordable Shared Living Communities
Co-living hubs near campuses offering private rooms with shared amenities at subsidized rates.
06
Integrated Housing + Transport
Bundled metro/bus pass discounts with housing contracts to reduce total living cost friction.
Conclusion

A Better Life Starts with Better Housing

Improving affordable housing is not only about reducing rent. It also reduces commute burden, improves well-being, increases productivity, and creates better quality of life for students and young professionals. When we solve housing, we unlock human potential.
